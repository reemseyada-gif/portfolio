The Balancing Act

Solving two-step equations by keeping both sides equal. A short interactive lesson that moves from idea, to hands-on exploration, to practice, to a quick check.

Level Grade 7–8 Time about 15 min Format self-paced Designed by Reem Eyada
1Learn 2Explore 3Practice 4Check
Stage 1 · Learn

An equation is a balanced scale

The equals sign means both sides weigh the same. To find the unknown, you may do anything you like to one side, as long as you do exactly the same to the other side. The scale stays level, and the equation stays true.

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Explain why an operation must be applied to both sides of an equation.
  • Solve equations of the form ax + b = c in two steps.
  • Check a solution by substituting it back into the equation.

Step 1: undo the constant

Remove the number added to or subtracted from x, using the opposite operation.

Step 2: undo the coefficient

Divide both sides by the number multiplying x.

2x + 5 = 17Start
2x + 5 − 5 = 17 − 5Subtract 5 from both sides
2x = 12Simplify
x = 6Divide both sides by 2
2(6) + 5 = 17 ✓Check by substituting

Behind the design

Instructional design choices

Concrete before abstract

The balance scale gives students a physical model of equality before they handle symbols alone.

Feedback on every mistake

Wrong moves are explained, not just marked wrong, so each error becomes a teaching moment.

Gradual release

Worked example, guided exploration, then independent practice and a short assessment.

Data ready

Attempts, hints and quiz scores can be logged to feed a learning analytics dashboard.
