How I work

Concrete first

Children meet a number as things they can count, move and group before they meet it as a symbol.

Play with purpose

Every game maps to a lesson objective. Feedback is immediate, kind, and uses the child's own name.

Evidence after

Results come back as data, so I can see which ideas landed and which need another lesson.

“A child understands a number once they can hold it.”

I'm Reem Eyada, a mathematics teacher who designs digital learning. I build games and modules in Arabic and English for primary classrooms, test them with real children, and read the data to decide what to teach next.

The games on this page are free for teachers and families to use.
